# Course synthesis and next steps

Use this page after the final presentations to identify what you can now do
independently and what you want to practice next.

## What changed in your workflow?

Write short responses to these questions:

1. What is one task you can now complete without step-by-step instructions?
2. When code produces a result, what evidence do you check before trusting it?
3. When would you choose a notebook, and when would you choose a script?
4. What is one responsible-computing choice you made in the final project?
5. What is one specific skill you want to practice in your next project?

## Keep the project usable

Before leaving the course, confirm that the final repository explains its
environment, data access, run order, and known limitations. Save the website
URL and repository URL somewhere your team can find them later.

## A small next step

Choose one realistic follow-up action: rerun the project from a clean start,
improve one figure, turn one repeated notebook task into a script, document a
limitation more clearly, or apply the same workflow to a new question.
