Label it yourself

Week 10

Two people label the same r/AmItheAsshole posts on a labeling sheet. There is no answer key, so you compare the two labelers with each other and with Gemini, and measure how much of their agreement is more than chance.

On r/AmItheAsshole, people describe a conflict and ask whether they were in the wrong. The label here is one no single word gives away: does the poster admit they did something wrong?

Gemini is gemini-3.5-flash-lite, with the same definition you use. Its labels are from one run.

Summary

  

    
    
      
  • When there is no answer key, two people label the same texts with the same definition. Agreement is the share of texts they give the same label.
    • 
      
  • Label independently: Labeler B should not see Labeler A's labels.
    • 
      
  • When one label is rare, two labelers agree often by chance. Cohen's kappa subtracts that: 1 is perfect agreement, 0 is what chance would give.
    • 
      
  • Where labelers disagree, the definition usually has no rule for that kind of text. Add one before you label more.
