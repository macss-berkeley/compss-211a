Label it yourself

Week 10

Label comments and posts from r/AmItheAsshole by hand. Then compare your labels with the commenter's own verdict, with Gemini, and with the person next to you.

On r/AmItheAsshole, people describe a conflict and ask whether they were in the wrong. Most comments start with a verdict: NTA (not the asshole) or YTA (you're the asshole). Here that first word is removed, so you can label the comment yourself and then check.

Posts have no word that gives the answer. For posts you label something else: does the poster admit they did something wrong? Gemini is gemini-3.5-flash-lite, and its labels are from one run.

1.Does the comment say the poster was in the wrong?

Read each comment and pick a label. The answer is the verdict word the commenter wrote at the start.
